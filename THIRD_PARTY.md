# Third-party software

## dnsproxy

Release builds bundle `bin/dnsproxy`, an unmodified binary from
[AdguardTeam/dnsproxy](https://github.com/AdguardTeam/dnsproxy), licensed under the
[Apache License 2.0](https://github.com/AdguardTeam/dnsproxy/blob/master/LICENSE).
The exact version and SHA-256 of the release archive are pinned in `scripts/dnsproxy.pin`; its license text ships as
`bin/dnsproxy.LICENSE`.
